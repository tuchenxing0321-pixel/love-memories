# Memories v2

## Scope

Target: `memories.html`. Mode: Experience with secondary Operate states. Primary visitors are nono and txx; shared visitors can browse public content. The page must preserve real photo and event CRUD, continuous dual-rail motion, hover/focus pause, browser persistence, and the existing public/private truth.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Memory Horizon makes the photographs one continuous horizon and lets the timeline answer the image a visitor stops on. It refuses the category-default oversized title followed by a row of equal gallery cards.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm paper-white ground, near-black ink, thin mineral rules, and one muted wine accent for active state and risk. Photographs sit as uncoated prints with mixed widths, minimal shadow, precise date labels, and controls that recede until needed.

**STORY:** Visitors first encounter living photographs, pause one to recover its date and event, then continue into a narrative timeline. Owners can manage safely, undo deletion, add events, and back up without leaving the archive’s calm reading rhythm.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact 64px archive header leads into a restrained title band. Two full-width, offset photo rails dominate the viewport. A selected card carries one date and event line. Visible pause and photo-management controls sit at the upper right. The timeline’s first event begins before the fold.

**FORM:** Chosen form is “记忆地平线”, fourth in the ordered structural list and the assigned lead. Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/memory-horizon.webp`. Reference: `seed:4b6734cb576aebf3`.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

Responsive from 390px mobile through 1440px desktop; no global minimum width. Motion must stop through a visible control and `prefers-reduced-motion`. Every destructive action exposes its target and offers recovery. Empty states must invite real content without pretending examples are personal memories. The viewer keeps complete images visible without heavy black surrounds.
